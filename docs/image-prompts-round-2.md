# Round two: filling the thin projects

A short list on purpose. Eighteen frames across six projects, weighted towards
the interior work, which is where the site is currently thinnest: only four of
the twenty projects sit under Interior Design, and two of those have a single
photograph each.

Same drop-in rule as before. Put the file at
`src/assets/projects/<slug>/<role>.webp`, commit, push. Nothing else to change.

Export WebP quality 82, 1920x1080 for before/after pairs, 1600x1200 for parts.
Shoot each before and after from the same position and focal length: the site
cross-fades them under a drag handle, and a matched viewpoint is what sells it.

## House style, append to every prompt

```
documentary architectural photography, shot on a 35mm full-frame camera with a tilt-shift lens, verticals corrected, natural available light, realistic materials with visible texture and slight weathering, muted neutral colour grade of white, warm grey and coffee brown, unpolished and true-to-life
```

### Negative prompt

```
glossy stock-photo look, HDR, over-saturated sky, lens flare, plastic sheen, mirror-perfect surfaces, showroom gloss, CGI render look, fisheye distortion, leaning verticals, watermark, text, logos, cartoon, illustration, oversharpened, heavy vignette
```

Every `before` frame carries two or three architects in hi-vis vests and hard
hats, mid-task over drawings, never looking at the camera. Overcast beats golden
hour. Dust, site mud and protective sheeting are all wanted.

## 1. Marble Line Flagship, Milan (Interior Design)

Currently two frames and no details. A single block of Carrara runs through the
shop as window sill, counter and stair, under the existing masonry vaults.

**`part-1.webp` — Continuous veining**

```
Architectural detail: the veining of a single Carrara marble slab running unbroken from window sill into counter top inside a small Milanese shop, vaults above, indirect light washing the ceiling with no visible fittings, close square-on framing.
```

**`part-2.webp` — The turn**

```
Architectural detail: the point where a solid Carrara counter turns and becomes the first treads of a stair, cut from one block, joint lines and arris visible, shop interior beyond, even indirect light.
```

**`part-3.webp` — Washed vaults**

```
Interior view upward into restored masonry vaults washed with concealed indirect light, no light fitting anywhere in frame, pale plaster and stone, retail floor below out of focus.
```

**`before.webp` — Strip-out**

```
Construction-stage interior of a stripped Milanese retail unit, masonry vaults newly exposed, old shopfitting removed, dust sheets and trestles, chalk setting-out lines on the floor. Two architects in hi-vis and hard hats crouched over a drawing, one gesturing along the chalk line.
```
